Features

Forensic-grade detection, made simple

The same techniques human-rights researchers use to uncover state spyware, in an app anyone can run.

Mercenary spyware

Matches against public indicators for NSO Pegasus, Predator, Candiru, QuaDream, Operation Triangulation, NoviSpy and more.

Stalkerware

Detects 190+ commercial monitoring apps that a partner, ex or employer may have hidden on the phone to read messages and track location.

Trojans & malware

Scores every app on behaviour: accessibility and device-admin abuse, SMS interception, overlays, hidden icons, fake "system" apps.

Deep forensic scan

Analyses a full Android bug report or an encrypted iPhone backup with MVT: message links, browsing history, process and data-usage records.

One-click removal

Tick a threat and remove it. Apps lose their access and are uninstalled. Anything that needs a tap on the phone is explained step by step.

Security check-up

Bootloader, root/jailbreak, patch age, Play Protect, proxies, private DNS, VPNs, profiles, Developer Mode, each with a plain-language fix.

Clear report

Device brand, model, OS and patch level; threats, actions taken and prioritised recommendations, as HTML you can print or share.

Optional cloud check

Add a free VirusTotal or MalwareBazaar key to check sideloaded apps against 70+ antivirus engines. Only fingerprints are sent.

Nothing to install

A single .exe with adb and the threat database built in. No Python, no SDK, no root or jailbreak needed.

How it works

Three steps, no expertise needed

PhoneSentry walks you through connecting the phone and explains every result in plain language.

Connect your phone

Plug it in with a USB cable. PhoneSentry detects it and shows the brand and model.

Android: turn on USB debugging and tap "Allow". iPhone: install Apple Devices and tap "Trust".

Scan

Quick scan in about a minute, or a deep forensic scan for Pegasus-grade coverage.

Deep scan: 5-10 min on Android, 15-40 min on iPhone.

Review, remove, report

See what was found and why, remove threats with one click, and get a full report with recommendations.

Reports are saved to Documents\PhoneSentry Reports.

Privacy & ethics

Built for the phone's owner, nobody else

🔒 Your data stays with you

  • Everything runs locally on your PC. There's no account and no telemetry.
  • Raw bug reports and backups are deleted after the scan unless you keep them.
  • Serial numbers are masked in reports.
  • Cloud lookups are off by default and send only app fingerprints.

🤝 Consent first

  • Every scan starts with an informed-consent confirmation.
  • Only scan a phone you own, or one whose owner is present and agrees.
  • For stalkerware, PhoneSentry warns that removal may alert whoever installed it, and points to support services.
Honest limits. A clean result lowers the likelihood of known threats but can't prove a phone is uncompromised: brand-new spyware may leave no visible trace. If you're a journalist, activist or official and believe you're targeted, contact Amnesty Security Lab or Access Now's helpline. Both help for free.
FAQ

Questions, answered

Can PhoneSentry remove Pegasus?

On Android, infected apps can be uninstalled. On iPhone, Pegasus-class implants live in system memory and no tool can delete them. When indicators are found, PhoneSentry tells you how to preserve evidence, where to get expert help, and how to protect yourself: update iOS, restart daily and turn on Lockdown Mode.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

It's free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence. There are no ads, accounts or telemetry. The code is on GitHub for anyone to inspect.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

PhoneSentry isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns on first launch. Click More info › Run anyway. You can check that your download is genuine by comparing its SHA-256 with the one published below and on the GitHub release (Get-FileHash PhoneSentry.exe), or build it yourself from source.

Do I need to root or jailbreak my phone?

No. Android only needs USB debugging, which you can turn off again afterwards. iPhone only needs you to tap "Trust".

Will the scan change anything on my phone?

No. Scanning is read-only. Nothing is changed unless you choose to remove a threat. On iPhone, the deep scan may offer to turn on encrypted backups, with a password you choose.

Why does the iPhone deep scan take so long?

It makes a full local backup (like iTunes) and analyses it. That takes time and free disk space roughly equal to the data on the phone. The backup is deleted afterwards.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. PhoneSentry is Windows-only for now. On macOS and Linux you can use MVT directly from the command line.

The scan flagged an app I trust. What now?

"Items to review" are flagged by behaviour, not confirmed malware. If you installed the app on purpose from a trusted source, keep it and leave it unticked. Known threats (spyware/stalkerware indicator matches) are marked Critical.
