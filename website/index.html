Features

Forensic detection without the forensics training

PhoneSentry packages the methods human-rights researchers use to find state spyware into an app anyone can run.

Mercenary spyware

Matches against published indicators for NSO Pegasus, Predator, Candiru, QuaDream, Operation Triangulation, NoviSpy and others.

Stalkerware

Finds more than 190 commercial monitoring apps that someone with access to the phone may have installed to read messages and track location.

Trojans and malware

Scores every app on behaviour: accessibility and device-admin abuse, SMS interception, screen overlays, hidden icons, fake system apps.

Deep forensic scan

Analyses a full Android bug report or an encrypted iPhone backup with MVT, covering message links, browser history and process records.

Threat removal

Select a threat and remove it. Apps lose their permissions and are uninstalled. Steps that need a tap on the phone are explained.

Security check-up

Bootloader, root and jailbreak, patch age, Play Protect, proxies, private DNS, VPNs, profiles and Developer Mode, each with a fix.

Readable report

Brand, model, OS and patch level; what was found and why; what was removed; and a prioritised list of what to do next.

Optional cloud lookup

Add a free VirusTotal or MalwareBazaar key to check sideloaded apps against 70+ engines. Only file fingerprints are sent.

Nothing to set up

One executable with adb and the threat database included. No Python, no SDK, and no root or jailbreak on the phone.

How it works

Three steps

The app guides you through connecting the phone and explains each result in plain language.

  1. Connect the phone

    Plug it in with a USB cable. PhoneSentry detects it and shows the brand and model.

    Android: enable USB debugging and tap Allow. iPhone: install Apple Devices and tap Trust.

  2. Run a scan

    A quick scan takes about a minute. The deep forensic scan adds full spyware forensics.

    Deep scan: 5 to 10 minutes on Android, 15 to 40 on iPhone.

  3. Review and act

    See what was found and why, remove threats, and open the report with recommendations.

    Reports are saved to Documents\PhoneSentry Reports.

Privacy

Built for the phone's owner

Your data stays on your PC

  • Everything runs locally. No account and no telemetry.
  • Raw bug reports and backups are deleted after the scan unless you keep them.
  • Serial numbers are masked in reports.
  • Cloud lookups are off by default and send only app fingerprints.

Consent comes first

  • Every scan starts with an informed-consent confirmation.
  • Scan only a phone you own, or one whose owner is present and agrees.
  • For stalkerware, the app warns that removal may alert whoever installed it and points to support services.
Limits. A clean result lowers the likelihood of known threats but can't prove a phone is uncompromised: new spyware may leave no visible trace. If you are a journalist, activist or official and believe you are targeted, contact Amnesty Security Lab or the Access Now helpline. Both help for free.
FAQ

Common questions

Can PhoneSentry remove Pegasus?

On Android, infected apps can be uninstalled. On iPhone, Pegasus-class implants run in system memory and no tool can delete them. When indicators are found, the report explains how to preserve evidence, where to get expert help, and how to protect yourself: update iOS, restart daily and turn on Lockdown Mode.

Is it free?

Yes. It's open source under the GPL-3.0 licence, with no ads, accounts or telemetry. The code is on GitHub.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

The executable isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns on first launch. Choose More info, then Run anyway. To confirm the file is genuine, compare its SHA-256 (Get-FileHash PhoneSentry.exe) with the checksum below, or build it from source.

Do I need to root or jailbreak the phone?

No. Android needs USB debugging, which you can switch off afterwards. iPhone only needs you to tap Trust.

Does scanning change anything on the phone?

No. Scanning is read-only, and nothing changes unless you choose to remove a threat. On iPhone, the deep scan may offer to turn on encrypted backups with a password you choose.

Why does the iPhone deep scan take so long?

It makes a full local backup, like iTunes does, then analyses it. That needs time and free disk space roughly equal to the data on the phone. The backup is deleted afterwards.

Is there a version for Mac or Linux?

Not yet. On macOS and Linux you can run MVT directly from the command line.

A trusted app was flagged. What should I do?

Items under "review" are flagged by behaviour, not confirmed as malware. If you installed the app on purpose from a source you trust, keep it. Matches against known spyware or stalkerware are marked Critical.
